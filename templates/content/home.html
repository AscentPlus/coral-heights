Welcome to Coral Heights

Where Kerala Heritage
Meets Modern Comfort

Nestled in the cultural heart of Ambalapuzha, Alappuzha, Hotel Coral Heights blends timeless Kerala hospitality with thoughtfully appointed modern luxury. Steps away from the revered Sree Krishna Temple and minutes from the tranquil backwaters, every stay is crafted for warmth, safety, and serenity.

Whether you are visiting for a family pilgrimage, a relaxing backwater escape, or a grand celebration, enjoy spacious rooms, authentic coastal dining at The Pepper Tree, and attentive service around the clock.
